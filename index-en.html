Academic Background

As an independent candidate, I have pursued my schooling in recent years through a private online high school that is not under formal contract with the French state but is officially declared to the French authorities and operates under the dministrative supervision of the French Ministry of National Education. This ensures that the curriculum followed complies with national standards. The school is called CNEEL (Centre National d’Enseignement E-Learning).This institution delivers the same curriculum as the French national education system, with courses conducted via videoconference, fixed schedules, supervised assessments, regular assignments, and an organization comparable to that of a traditional in-person high school.This educational path resulted from a family relocation abroad. Despite this situation, I made the deliberate decision to remain within the French educational system in order to pursue higher education in France.

Throughout my academic journey, I have completed all required stages of schooling and sat for the official examinations, from the Brevet to the Baccalauréat preliminary examinations, achieving excellent results (“mention très bien” in the Brevet: 357.5/400, and an average of 18.5/20 in the Baccalauréat preliminary exams). I have also maintained an average of around 18/20 each term during the last years of my schooling.These results reflect my dedication, adaptability, and academic rigor, as well as my strong mastery of the expectations of the French national curriculum despite an atypical educational path.
