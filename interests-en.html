Interests

In the field of programming, I independently develop my skills through personal projects and online courses such as Harvard’s CS50 and MIT modules, as well as the PIX certification (obtained with a score of 787 – Expert Level), reflecting my curiosity and strong interest in digital technologies.

Passionate about travel and languages, I am trilingual (French, English, and Spanish) and hold the First Certificate in English (FCE), with an intermediate level in Portuguese. My involvement in cultural events promoting exchanges between France, Brazil, and Africa has strengthened my intercultural communication skills and broadened my global perspective.

In the field of arts and music, I practice Afro-Brazilian dance and percussion, activities that nurture my creativity and interest in diverse cultures.

I also practice sports, particularly tennis and swimming, disciplines that have taught me perseverance and discipline.

Finally, I am interested in other areas such as reading and digital culture, and I regularly participate in MOOCs. My civic and community engagement, including volunteer work at the Alliance Française of Salvador da Bahia and participation in various cultural events, as well as mentoring primary school students, has helped me develop responsibility, teaching skills, and strong organizational abilities.
